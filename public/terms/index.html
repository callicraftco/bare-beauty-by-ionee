Booking terms

Last updated October 22, 2026

These terms apply whenever you book with Bare Beauty by Ionee, online or in person. By booking, you agree to them. If anything here isn't clear, ask me before you book.

Booking and confirmation

  • Online, you pick your services and a time. Depending on how booking is set up, your appointment is either confirmed right away or sent to me as a request that I confirm by text or email.
  • A request isn't an appointment until I confirm it. If I can't do that time, I'll suggest another.
  • Please give a mobile number where I can reach you.

Changes, cancellations and late arrivals

  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or change your appointment.
  • You can cancel online with the link in your confirmation until 24 hours before your appointment. After that, please text me.
  • If you're more than 15 minutes late, I may need to shorten your service or reschedule it, so the next client isn't kept waiting.
  • If you miss appointments without telling me, I may ask you to book by text instead of online.
  • If I need to cancel or move your appointment, I'll tell you as soon as I can and help you find a new time.

Payment

  • Payment is due at your appointment.
  • Prices are listed on this website and may change. If you add or change services during your visit, the price changes to match.

Your health and safety

Before your service, tell me about:

  • Allergies or sensitivities, including to adhesives, latex, wax or skincare ingredients
  • Skin conditions, sunburn, or broken or irritated skin
  • Medications that affect your skin, like retinoids, isotretinoin (Accutane) or blood thinners
  • Pregnancy, recent cosmetic treatments, or anything else I should know

I may change or decline a service if it isn't safe for you, and lash services may need a patch test first. Results vary from person to person. Mild redness or sensitivity after a facial, wax or lash service is common. Follow the aftercare I give you, contact me if anything worries you, and see a doctor if you have a serious reaction.

Clients under 18

Clients under 18 need a parent or guardian to book for them and give consent. A parent or guardian may need to stay for the appointment.

Photos

I love sharing my work, but I only post photos that show you with your permission. You can ask me to take a photo down at any time.

Respect and safety in the studio

I want the studio to feel calm and safe for everyone. I may decline or end a service if someone is abusive, unsafe or under the influence.

Using this website

Please book only for yourself, or for someone who agreed to it, and give accurate details. Don't misuse the booking system, for example with fake bookings or automated tools. The photos, text and branding on this site belong to Bare Beauty by Ionee. Please don't reuse them without permission.

Responsibility

I take care to provide every service safely and professionally. To the extent the law allows, Bare Beauty by Ionee isn't responsible for indirect losses, and its responsibility for any service is limited to the price you paid for it. Nothing in these terms takes away any rights you have that the law doesn't allow to be limited.

Changes to these terms

I may update these terms. The version on this page when you book is the one that applies to that booking.

The law that applies

These terms are governed by the laws of the state where the studio is located.
