Accessibility

Last reviewed October 22, 2026

I want everyone to be able to see the menu and book with ease, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard or zoom.

What this site does

  • It aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.
  • Every page works with a keyboard alone and has been checked with automated accessibility testing.
  • Text and buttons have strong color contrast.
  • Animations stop when your device's "reduce motion" setting is on.
  • Booking works on phones, tablets and computers, and pages still work when zoomed in.

Known limits

  • Instagram posts on the home page use Instagram's own descriptions when there are any. Some are less detailed.
  • Automated testing can't catch everything, so I welcome your feedback.

Need help, or found a problem?

If anything gets in your way, I'll book you directly and get it fixed. Use the contact details on the home page.

If you have questions about getting to or around the studio, ask me before your visit and I'll help you plan.
