Before you come

How to prep for each service, and how booking, changes and payment work.

How do I book?

Pick your services and a time on the booking page, then add your details. You'll hear from me by text or email to confirm.

How should I prepare for a facial?

Come with a clean face if you can. Pause retinol, exfoliating acids and scrubs for 3 days before. Let me know about allergies, skin conditions or medications when you book.

How should I prepare for waxing?

Hair should be about 1/4 inch long, roughly 2 to 3 weeks of growth. Skip exfoliating and retinol for 2 days before, and avoid tanning the day of your appointment.

How should I prepare for lashes?

Arrive with clean lashes: no mascara, lash curler or oily makeup remover that day. Plan to relax with your eyes closed for the whole appointment.

When should I book a fill?

Fills are for lashes done within the last 2 weeks. If it has been longer, you may need a full set.

What if I need to cancel or I'm running late?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or change your time. Use the link in your confirmation or text me. If you're more than 15 minutes late, I may need to shorten or reschedule your service.

How do I pay?

Payment is due at your appointment.
