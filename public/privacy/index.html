Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Bare Beauty by Ionee is a small beauty studio run by Ionee. This page explains what I collect when you use this website or book with me, why, and the choices you have. In short: I only collect what I need to take care of your appointments, I never sell it, and this site has no ads or tracking.

What I collect

When you book online, or when I book you myself:

  • Your name and mobile number, plus your email and Instagram handle if you share them
  • The services, day and time you pick, and whether it's your first visit
  • Anything you write in "Anything I should know?" This can include health details you choose to share, like allergies, skin conditions or medications.
  • That you agreed to my booking terms, and whether you asked for offer texts, with the date

As your esthetician, I also keep notes about your visits, such as your skin type, allergies, lash style and what you like. They help me treat you safely and the same way each time.

When you visit the site:

  • My website host, Netlify, handles your IP address and basic browser details to deliver pages and protect the site, like any website host.
  • To stop spam bookings, the booking system keeps a code made from your IP address with a secret key, so it can't be turned back into your address. It's deleted within two days.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies for visitors and uses no analytics, ads or tracking pixels. The home page saves one small note in your browser for the current tab, so its opening animation only plays once. It holds no personal information and goes away when you close the tab.

Fonts load from this site, not from Google or anyone else. Instagram posts on the home page are resized and served by this site's host, so Instagram doesn't learn that you visited. If you tap a post, you go to Instagram, and Instagram's own privacy policy applies there.

How I use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, change and remind you about appointments
  • To give you safe services and keep a record of what was done
  • To answer your questions
  • To send offer texts, only if you asked for them
  • To keep the site and booking system secure, and to meet legal and tax obligations

Texts and emails

Appointment texts come from me personally, from the studio phone. They aren't automated. If you give your email, confirmation and reminder emails are sent for me by an email delivery service.

Offer texts, like openings and specials, only go to clients who asked for them, by checking the box when booking or by telling me. If you've been in before, I may also text you when you're due for your next visit. Reply STOP or tell me anytime and I'll stop. Message and data rates may apply.

Who I share it with

I don't sell your information or share it for advertising. Only these handle it:

  • Netlify, which hosts this website and stores booking records
  • An email delivery service (currently Resend), if you gave your email
  • The calendar, email and cloud storage services I use to run my day and keep backups, such as Apple or Google. My calendar only shows your first name, last initial and services.
  • Anyone the law requires, such as in response to a valid legal request

The person who built and looks after this website can also reach the system to keep it running.

How long I keep it

I keep client records for 3 years after your last visit. Then I erase your name, contact details and notes, and keep only bookkeeping details like the date, service and price.

Your choices

You can ask me to:

  • Send you a copy of the information I have about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Erase your information. If you have an upcoming appointment, I'll cancel it first. Your details leave my booking system right away, and my calendar soon after. Older backup files and past emails can still hold them until those are deleted, and I keep both private.
  • Stop offer texts

Use the contact details on the home page. I'll answer within 30 days. I may need to confirm it's you first, for example by texting the number on your record.

Keeping it safe

The site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Booking records are stored privately with the website host. I see them through a password-protected studio panel, and so can the person who looks after the site. The appointment link in your confirmation is unique to you, so please don't share it.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information online. Clients under 18 need a parent or guardian to book for them.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top of this page.

Questions

Use the contact details on the home page.
