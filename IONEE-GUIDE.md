# Your studio panel

Your website has a private panel for bookings, clients and your menu. Open it at **yourdomain.com/admin** and sign in with your password.

Put it on your phone's home screen so it opens like an app: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

## Today

This is your main screen.

- **Finish setting up**: a short checklist for your first days. Tap Open next to each item.
- **Notices**: a heads-up when Instagram needs a look, or when old client records are about to be cleared.
- **Waiting for you**: online bookings that need a yes or no. Tap Confirm or Decline. Text opens your Messages app with a message already written. Change anything before you send it.
- **Today**: everyone coming in today. Tap a name for the details.
- **Reminders for tomorrow**: tap Text reminder for each client. It opens Messages with the reminder filled in and marks it as sent.
- **At a glance**: what's booked this week and what you've earned this month.
- **Due to come back**: clients who are due for a fill, facial or wax, based on their last visit. Tap Text to invite them back. Clients who said STOP to offer texts show "No offer texts" instead.

## Schedule

- Tap a day in the week strip to see that day. Use the arrows for other weeks.
- **Appointment**: add a booking from a text, a DM or a walk-in. Start typing a name to find an existing client. The open times for that day show as buttons.
- **Block time**: close off a vacation, an appointment or a lunch break so nobody can book it.
- **List** shows all requests, upcoming visits, past visits and cancellations, with search.

Tap any appointment to:

- Confirm, decline, mark it completed, mark a no-show, or cancel
- Change the time or the services
- Text, call or email the client
- Send a confirmation, reminder or thank-you text
- Keep private notes that only you see

## Clients

Everyone who books is added here. Tap a client to see their visits, what they've spent, and your notes. Good things to note: skin type, allergies, lash style and mapping, what they like.

- **Book [name]** starts a new appointment for them.
- **Offer texts** shows whether they said yes to texts about openings and offers, and when. If someone says yes in person, tap Mark as said yes. If anyone replies STOP, tap Mark as opted out right away.
- **Privacy**: if a client asks what you keep about them, tap Download their data and send them the file. If they ask you to delete it, tap Erase their details. Their name, phone, email and notes are removed for good. Dates, services and prices stay for your records.
- **Remove from list** is for duplicates and mistakes. It doesn't erase anything from their appointments.

## Menu

Change prices, times and descriptions. Your website updates within a few minutes after you tap Save menu.

- **Length for booking** is how long the appointment blocks on your calendar. Keep it accurate so you never get double-booked.
- **Remind to rebook after (days)** decides when a client shows up in Due to come back.
- **Clients can book it online**: turn off for services people should text you about.
- **Show on the website**: turn off to hide a service without deleting it.

## Settings

- **Business**: phone, email, the area you work in (shown on the site) and your address (only sent once a booking is confirmed). Your About text and an announcement bar for the top of the site, like "Booked through Nov 15".
- **Booking**: your weekly hours, whether you confirm each request or bookings confirm right away, cleanup time between clients, how much notice you need, how far ahead people can book, and your payment note.
- **Policies**: the questions and answers in "Before you come" on your site.
- **Texts**: the words your Text buttons fill in. The preview shows what a client will see.
- **Photos**: your portrait for the About section, and photos of your work. They show in "Recent work" whenever Instagram isn't connected or switched on.
- **Instagram**: your latest posts fill "Recent work" and update by themselves every few hours. Choose how many show (3 to 12), switch them off, or tap Update now right after you post. Connecting the first time needs a one-time setup from your web person.
- **Calendar**: a private link that puts your bookings in your phone's calendar, with steps for iPhone and Google, and a test for booking emails. To keep clients' details private, the calendar shows first names, last initials and services. Phone numbers and notes stay in the panel.
- **Privacy**: links to your privacy policy, booking terms and accessibility page, and how long to keep client records. Records of clients who haven't visited in that long are cleared on the 1st of each month.
- **Backup**: download a backup once a month and keep it in Google Drive.
- **Password**: choose your own password. Saving it signs out your other phones and computers.

## Your website's policy pages

Every page links to your Privacy policy, Booking terms and Accessibility page at the bottom, and clients agree to the booking terms when they book. Read them once, and tell your web person if anything doesn't match how you work, like your cancellation rule or how you handle clients under 18.

## Good habits

- Confirm requests the same day. Clients see "Waiting for confirmation" until you do.
- Block time off as soon as you know about it.
- Send offers and specials only to clients who said yes. A personal invite back is fine unless they've opted out. Mark anyone who replies STOP right away.
- Download a backup at the start of each month.
